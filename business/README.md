# Business validation package

This folder contains early-stage business materials for positioning the project as:

> **A secure age-verification system for automated sales in regulated sectors, with privacy by design and auditable traceability.**

The materials are intended for validation conversations with potential customers, sector associations, regulators, technology partners, accelerators and funding bodies.

## Contents

- `one-pager.md` — concise project overview.
- `pitch-deck-8-slides.md` — 8-slide pitch structure.
- `regulatory-dossier.md` — regulatory validation plan and key questions.
- `contacts-validation-map.md` — 20 public contacts and outreach strategy.
- `demo-video-plan.md` — script and structure for a short demo video.
- `mvp-budget.md` — MVP budget scenarios and funding path.
- `legal-product-memo-es.md` — Spanish legal/product framing memo for review.
- `rgpd-data-matrix.md` — preliminary RGPD/DPIA data map.
- `launch-plan-spain.md` — Spanish launch and validation roadmap.
- `questions-comisionado.md` — preparatory questions for the tobacco regulator.
- `dpo-legal-review-notes.md` — checklist for external DPO/legal review.
- `discovery-interview-script.md` — interview guide for sector discovery.
- `funding-shortlist.md` — prioritized Spanish funding/support routes.

## Recommended strategy

Do not start by selling “a tobacco kiosk”.

Start with the broader and stronger positioning:

**Compliance layer for regulated automated sales.**

The tobacco/estanco use case is useful because it is concrete and demanding, but the long-term opportunity is wider: age verification, identity assurance and audit-safe compliance for automated retail.
