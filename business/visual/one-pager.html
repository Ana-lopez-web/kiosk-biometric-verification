The problem

Automated sales of restricted products require more than a normal vending flow. Operators must verify legal age, reduce fraud and keep evidence of responsible sale while avoiding unnecessary privacy risk.

The solution

A modular verification layer for kiosks and self-service terminals: document-reading interface, certificate-validation interface, liveness concept, 1:1 face match, legal-age calculation and HMAC-based audit token.

Initial use case

Licensed tobacco retail premises in Spain. The long-term opportunity is broader: regulated vending, alcohol, pharmacy/parapharmacy, identity-sensitive access and other age-restricted environments.
